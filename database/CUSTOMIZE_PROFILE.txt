HOW TO SET YOUR PROFILE

=== METHOD 1: Using update_profile.sql (Recommended) ===

1. Open database/update_profile.sql in a text editor
2. Replace the following with YOUR information:
   - 'Your Full Name' → Your actual name
   - 'your.email@example.com' → Your email
   - '+251-XXX-XXX-XXX' → Your phone number
   - Update social media URLs (GitHub, LinkedIn, Twitter)
   
3. Customize skills, projects, experience, and education to match YOUR work
4. Import in phpMyAdmin:
   - Open phpMyAdmin
   - Select portfolio_db database
   - Click "SQL" tab
   - Copy and paste the updated update_profile.sql content
   - Click "Go"

=== METHOD 2: Using Admin Panel (After Setup) ===

1. Complete the basic setup (import portfolio_db.sql)
2. Start backend and frontend servers
3. Login to admin panel: http://localhost:3000/admin/login
   - Username: admin
   - Password: admin123
4. Navigate to each section and update:
   - Manage Profile → Update personal information
   - Manage Skills → Add your video editing skills
   - Manage Projects → Add your portfolio projects
   - Manage Education → Add your education
   - Manage Experience → Add your work experience
   - Manage Certificates → Add your certifications

=== METHOD 3: Direct SQL Update (Quick) ===

Run this SQL in phpMyAdmin (SQL tab):

UPDATE profile SET 
  full_name = 'YOUR NAME',
  title = 'YOUR TITLE',
  bio = 'YOUR BIO',
  email = 'YOUR EMAIL',
  phone = 'YOUR PHONE'
WHERE id = 1;

=== PROFILE FIELDS EXPLANATION ===

full_name: Your complete name
title: Your professional title (e.g., "Video Editor", "Content Creator")
bio: Short description about yourself (2-3 sentences)
objective: Your career goals and what you're seeking
phone: Contact phone number
email: Contact email address
location: City/Country where you're based
profile_image: Will be uploaded through admin panel
resume_file: Will be uploaded through admin panel
github_url: Your GitHub profile (if applicable)
linkedin_url: Your LinkedIn profile
twitter_url: Your Twitter/X profile

=== ADDING YOUR WORK SAMPLES ===

After updating your profile:

1. Upload Project Images:
   - Login to admin panel
   - Go to "Manage Projects"
   - Click "Add Project" or "Edit" existing
   - Upload thumbnail/screenshot of your video
   - Add YouTube/Vimeo video links

2. Upload Profile Photo:
   - Go to "Manage Profile"
   - Scroll to "Upload Profile Image"
   - Choose your professional photo
   - Save

3. Upload Resume/CV:
   - Go to "Manage Profile"
   - Scroll to "Upload Resume"
   - Choose your PDF resume
   - Save
